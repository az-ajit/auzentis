Human Potential Assessment — Pilot
Pilot programme. This platform currently covers ages 6–25 across seven foundational domains (Numerical, Verbal, Logical, Memory, Attention, Problem-Solving, Learning Preference). Results are explained in plain language with stated limitations — no score here labels a person's worth or future potential on its own.

Sign in

Participants aged 18+ sign in directly. A parent/guardian signs in to register and manage an under-18 participant.
